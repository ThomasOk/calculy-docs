Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Welcome to Calculy! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address - Used for account authentication and communication
  • Username - Your public display name on leaderboards
  • Password - Stored securely in encrypted format (we never see your actual password)

1.2 Gameplay Data

To provide the core features of Calculy, we collect:

  • Challenge attempts - Your answers, completion times, and scores
  • Performance metrics - Number of mistakes, time per problem
  • Challenge history - Dates and types of challenges completed
  • Anti-cheat validation data - Automated checks to ensure fair play

1.3 Device Information

We may collect basic technical information:

  • Device type - iOS or Android
  • App version - To ensure compatibility
  • Error logs - To diagnose and fix crashes (via Sentry)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide the service - Enable you to play challenges and track progress
  • Display leaderboards - Show rankings with usernames (not emails)
  • Prevent cheating - Validate scores and detect suspicious patterns
  • Improve the app - Analyze crash reports and user feedback
  • Send essential communications - Account-related emails (password resets, important updates)

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider.

Security Measures:

  • Encryption - All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Password hashing - Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt)
  • Access control - Only you can access your account data
  • Regular backups - Data is backed up to prevent loss

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We DO NOT sell your personal data to anyone.

Third-party services we use:

  • Supabase - Database and authentication (see Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry - Crash reporting and error monitoring (see Sentry Privacy Policy)

These services only receive the minimum data necessary to function (e.g., Sentry receives error logs, not your email).

5. Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy Laws)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data - Request a copy of all data we have about you
  • Correct your data - Update your username or email
  • Delete your account - Permanently remove all your data from our systems
  • Export your data - Download your challenge history
  • Withdraw consent - Stop using the app and request deletion

To exercise these rights, contact us at: toshtoshtoshdev@gmail.com

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts - We keep your data as long as your account exists
  • Deleted accounts - Data is permanently removed within 30 days of deletion request
  • Leaderboard data - Historical scores may be retained for integrity but will be anonymized

7. Children's Privacy

Calculy is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account, please contact us to delete it.

8. Local Data Storage

For offline functionality, we store some data locally on your device:

  • Practice mode results - Stored in AsyncStorage (local device storage)
  • Authentication tokens - To keep you logged in

This data is only accessible by the Calculy app and is automatically synced to the cloud when you create an account.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored on servers located outside your country. By using Calculy, you consent to this transfer. We ensure that all transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

11. Contact Us
